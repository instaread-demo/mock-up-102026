    Underway

    Early voting open

    Early voting for the Nov. 3 general election begins Oct. 19 and will continue through Nov. 2 at the Jefferson County Courthouse. Early voting will be open from 8 a.m.-6 p.m. weekdays (with the exception of Nov. 2, when it closes at 5 p.m.), and 10 a.m.-4 p.m. Saturdays. It will be closed Sundays. Voters will go to the polls on Nov. 3 when polls will be open from 7:30 a.m. to 7:30 p.m. Sample ballots and more details are available on the Voter View website at https://www.voterview.ar-nova.org/VoterView

    PBPD sets Domestic Violence Walk 2026

    October is Domestic Violence Awareness Month and the Pine Bluff Police Department invites the community to join them in the 2026 Domestic Violence Walk. This event will take place from noon to 2 p.m. Oct. 20 in front of Pine Bluff City Hall, 200 E. Eighth Ave. This is a free community event open to the general public, according to Stuff in The Bluff.com.

    Retired teachers plan conference in Pine Bluff

    Arkansas Retired Teachers Association will have its Area 5 Fall Conference on Oct. 20 at the Arkansas River Education Service Cooperative, 912 W. Sixth Ave. Jefferson County Retired Teachers Association will host the conference, according to a news release. The event is scheduled from 10 a.m. to 2:30  p.m., according to a Facebook post. For registration information, contact Vickie Liddell at jamesvickieldd@sbcglobal.net. The deadline for registration is Oct. 1.

    Ivy Center plans Girl/Guy Talk

    The Ivy Center for Education will hold its Girl/Guy Talk on Oct. 20. The workshop will be held from 6-7:30 p.m. at the Generator, 435 S. Main St. The featured wrap up speaker will be Judge Earnest Brown. The talk will be presented with the women of Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority Inc. (the Alpha Rho Chapter at UAPB), the men of Delta Sigma Lambda Graduate Chapter of Alpha Phi Alpha Fraternity Inc., and the men of Pi Boule of Sigma Pi Phi Fraternity Inc. Students in grades 7-12 are encouraged to attend. Snacks will be provided. Those who cannot in person may join by Zoom with ID 856 8296 4187 and Password 351061. Details: mattie1908@gmail.com.

    Church news deadline set

    Church news is printed in The Commercial on Fridays. The deadline to submit church announcements is noon Tuesdays. Pastors, ministers or other interested writers for the Devotional Column may also submit articles for consideration. Articles should be emailed to Sandra Hope at shope@pbcommercial.com.

    Through Tuesday, Oct. 20

    Extension homemakers to host pecan, peanut sale 

    The Jefferson County Extension Homemakers are selling fresh 2026 shelled pecans and double dipped chocolate covered peanuts as its annual fundraiser, according to a news release. Prices this year are: 1 pound bag, shelled fancy pecan halves — $17 and 1 pound chocolate double dipped peanuts — $15. The deadline for orders is Oct. 20.  Make checks payable to Jefferson County Extension Homemakers and mail to: Linda Murray, Treasurer-Elect; 1003 Deer Run N.; Pine Bluff, AR 71603. Order forms are available at the Jefferson County Cooperative Extension Service, 500 S. Idaho St. in Pine Bluff, or by email to Mary Ann Kizer, Jefferson County Family and Consumer Sciences agent, at mkizer@uada.edu. Details: Mary Ann Kizer, 870-534-1033.

    Thursday, Oct. 22

    Chamber sets Farmers’ Appreciation fish fry

    The Pine Bluff Regional Chamber of Commerce will host the 74th annual Farmers Appreciation Fish Fry from 5-7 p.m. Oct. 22 at Hestand Stadium. Tickets are $25 for members and $30 for non members. Sponsorships are available for $400, according to the Chamber newsletter. King Kat will cater the catfish and chicken Details: Chamber at 870-535-0110  or Facebook.

    Friday, Oct. 23

    ‘Much Ado About Murder’ — Oct. 23–25

    The ARTx3 Campus will present the mystery-comedy “Much Ado About Murder” at 7 p.m. Oct. 23-24 and Oct. 25 in the Adam B. Robinson Jr. Black Box Theater at ART WORKS on Main, 627 S. Main St. Audience interaction is built into a plot filled with suspicious characters, dark humor and plenty of twists. “Rich but rotten Carlton Larraby enjoys punctuating his family’s lives with macabre little surprises. His latest joke is a Halloween party where everyone arrives dressed as a Shakespearean character,” according to a news release. Tickets are $18 and may be purchased at artx3.org/all-events/much-ado-about-murder, by calling or visiting the center in person.  

    Saturday, Oct. 24

    New Community changes pantry date

    New Community Church, 321 N. Birch St., will give away food Oct. 24 from 8-9 a.m. The date was changed from Oct. 17. Food will be given out from the pantry in the fellowship hall. The community is invited to attend, according to a spokesman. 

    Breast Cancer awareness 5K walk/run set

    The 12th annual Breast Cancer Awareness events in memory of Billie Jean “BJ” Jackson will contine Oct. 24. The 5K Walk/Run will begin at 9 a.m. at the Lake Saracen Pavilion, 200 Lake Saracen Drive. There will be free health screenings, live entertainment, vendors, a Kids Zone and fellowship. The walk/run registration fee is $40 for ages 12 and up, which includes a commemorative T-shirt. The community is invited to participate, according to Stuff in The Bluff.com. Details: https://www.bjlegacy.org/registration  or email bjjacksonfightcurelegacy@yahoo.com.

    One Vision of Queen to appear at Saracen

    One Vision of Queen featuring Marc Martel will perform at the Saracen Event Center at 8 p.m. Oct. 24. The performance is the “world’s most spectacular Queen tribute show,” according to a news release. “Known for his extraordinary vocal ability, Martel frequently drew comparisons to the legendary Freddie Mercury — a resemblance that would ultimately change the course of his career,” according to the release. Tickets sales began at $29, plus applicable fees, and go on sale June 26 at 10 a.m. through Ticketmaster.

    Locals among Black Hall of Fame honorees 

    The Arkansas Black Hall of Fame will induct seven honorees for their outstanding achievements on Oct. 24 at 7:30 p.m. at the Robinson Center Performance Hall in Little Rock. Tickets are available now at ticketmaster.com and arblackhalloffame.org. Honorees are The 2026 inductees include: Hazell Reed — Former dean and vice chancellor at the University of Arkansas at Pine Bluff; Dr. Raymond P. Miller (posthumous) — physician, scientist, and graduate of Arkansas AM&N College (now UAPB); Rebecca “Becky” Finney and Darrell Finney — Visionary entrepreneurs and philanthropic leaders; Frank A. James — renowned educator and civil rights icon; Joe Marcus Johnson — NBA legend and philanthropist; and Shirley N. Weber — the 32nd Secretary of State for the State of California.

Kriston Wilson, pastor of New Jerusalem Missionary Baptist Church. A special invitation is extended to members, former members and friends to join in the celebration. The theme is “Standing on the Shoulders, Walking in the Promise” (Psalm 105:8.) The Rev. Kirby L. Gulley is pastor of Eighth Avenue church. 

    WH church sets fall fest

    First Baptist Church of White Hall, 8708 Dollarway Road, will hold a fall fest from 4-6 p.m. Oct. 25. The event will include games, candy, other foods, and inflatables, according to a flyer.

    Barraque Street church hosts Women’s Day

    Barraque Street Missionary Baptist Church, 1800 W. Pullen St.,  will celebrate its annual Women’s Day at 11 a.m. Oct. 25. The theme is “Still Here: Resilient, Restored & Renewed,” (II Cor. 4:8-9.) The guest speaker will be the Rev.     Golden Hour: Movie Day (‘Hocus Pocus’) —  Oct. 30

This event is part of ARTx3’s Golden Hour programming for ages 50 and older. Golden Hour events are free, but registration is required. Register by Oct. 27 at artx3.org/all-events/golden-hour-movie-day or call the center.

    Through Friday, Oct. 30

    Lincoln County Angel Tree registration open

    Applications for the Lincoln County Angel Tree are available in Star City at the Department of Human Services Office. Registration is open through Oct. 30. The Lincoln County Angel Tree is for youth 0-12 years old. Toys for Tots will be providing toys and other items for qualifying families with children up to 18. The agency will assist older youth who have siblings covered by the program, according to a news release from Matthew Yarnell, Toys for Tots coordinator in Cleveland, Dallas, Jefferson, and Lincoln counties. Details: Mandy Horner at 870-628-4221, ext. 133; Dana Weast, 870-628-4221, ext. 114, or visit DHS, 101 W. Wiley St., Star City.

    Shoulder closures set on Interstate 530

    Motorists should expect right shoulder closures on Interstate 530 (I-530) northbound between Exit 39 in Pine Bluff and the Grant/Pulaski County line north of Exit 20 in Redfield now through Oct. 30. The closures will occur periodically Monday through Friday from 7 a.m. to 6 p.m., according to a news release.

    The White Hall Fire Department will host trick or treating on Halloween beginning at 6 p.m. Oct. 31. “Come see us at Station 1 (on Anderson by the Library) at 6 p.m. until we run out of candy,” according to a Facebook post. “Come take pictures with firefighters, a fire truck and get some candy. Can’t wait to see all the awesome costumes this year!” 

    Highway 15 fire officers set trunk or treat

    The Highway 15 South Volunteer Fire Department, 10672 U.S. 63 South, will conduct a trunk or treat event from 6-9 p.m. Oct. 31. Details: 870-592-2018, according to a flyer.

    Police plan fall festival

    The Pine Bluff Police Department Fall Festival will be held from 6-9 p.m. Oct. 31 at the Pine Bluff Convention Center. The event will include free candy, other refreshments, a costume contest, and bingo. “You won’t want to miss this. Fun and fright begin at 6 p.m. Wear your best costume and be ready for a chilling night of prizes, games and, of course, candy. This is a FREE event open to everyone,” according to a Facebook post.

    Sheriff’s office, churches set trunk or treat 

    The Jefferson County Sheriff’s Office has teamed up with New St Hurricane Missionary Baptist Church and Old Saint James Missionary Baptist Church to host a family-oriented trunk or treat. The event will be held Oct. 30 from 6-8 p.m. at New St. Hurricane, 3319 S. Ohio St. “Bring the kids, wear your favorite costumes and come enjoy candy, fellowship and community fun,” according to a Facebook post.

    Learning Lounge plans fall festival

    The Learning Lounge Academy, 9210 Sulphur Springs Road, invites the community to its Spooky Stories Fall Festival from 6-8 p.m. Oct. 30. It will be an evening of games, stories, candy, and more. Free stuffed animals will be given to the first 100 children. “Bring the whole family and help us celebrate spooky stories and brighter futures! ” Participants are asked to wear appropriate costumes only, according to a Facebook post.

    Through Saturday, Oct. 31

    Next Gen STEAM Exhibition Series underway

    The ARTX3 Campus is hosting the Next Gen STEAM Exhibition Series for four weeks. Visitors may drop into the Creative Construction Zone during regular visitor hours (10 a.m. to 5 p.m. Tuesday through Friday, and 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. Saturday). One segment is the Bird in a Cage series. It’s a hands-on science activity that explores air, motion and observation. Participants will create a simple model of a bird inside a paper “cage” and investigate how movement, air, or visual effects can make the bird appear to move or change position. This activity will encourage participants to make predictions, observe closely, and explain what you see using evidence, according to a news release.

    Teen Court seeks volunteers

    The Teen Court Volunteer Program of Jefferson and Lincoln counties is giving high school students a chance to step inside the courtroom, experience the justice system firsthand, and develop leadership skills that will last a lifetime. Students entering ninth through 12th grades are invited to participate. Recruitment is being held Aug. 3 through Oct. 31. Orientation will be held Sept. 1 from 4:15-5:30 p.m. “Whether your dream is to become a judge, attorney, law enforcement officer, educator, elected official or simply become a more confident and informed leader, Teen Court is where leadership begins,” according to a Facebook post. Details: 870-541-5455.

    Sunday, Nov. 1

    Toys for Tots to accept donations

    Toys for Tots will hold a toy drive from 8 a.m. to 3 p.m. Nov. 1 at Leon’s Catfish, 3801 Chapel Village, Suite 18. Toys for Tots seeks to raise donations for youth in need and at-risk youth in the area for Christmas. The program benefits children in Jefferson, Cleveland, Dallas and Lincoln counties. The agency accepts new toys, books, games or financial donations. Details: pine-bluff-ar.toysfortots.org.

    New Faith observes pastoral anniversary

    New Faith Missionary Baptist Church, 3800 S. Fir St., will celebrate the 15th Pastoral Anniversary of the Rev. S.E. Shaw and Cassandra Shaw at 3 p.m. Nov. 1. The guest speaker will be the Rev. Fred Wisemon, pastor of Cherry Hill Missionary Baptist Church in Moscow. The theme is “I will give you shepherds after my own heart, who will lead you with knowledge and understanding” (Jer. 3:15.) The church will commemorate 15 years of faithful leadership, dedication and service.

    Thursday, Nov. 5

    City plans paper shredding event

    The community is invited to participate in a free fall paper shredding event from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. Nov. 5 at Saracen Landing. Paper shredding allows people to declutter and protect their personal information. There’s a limit of 10 boxes per person or organization. All items should be sorted before bringing them to the site where papers will be shredded securely, according to a flyer. The event is hosted by Pine Bluff/Jefferson County Clean and Beautiful Commission. Details: Clean and Beautiful office, 870-543-4901.

    Faith church begins Revelation Bible Study

    Faith Community Church, 3703 Ryburn Road, will host “Revelation Bible Study, a deeper look at the end; a stronger walk for tomorrow.” The Bible study will begin at 1 p.m. Nov. 5 and the featured speaker will be the Rev. Don Taylor, pastor of Immanuel Baptist Church. The study will continue at 1 p.m. each Thursday for approximately 13 weeks, according to a spokesman. There will be an exploration of the scriptures and discussions. All are welcome to attend. The Rev. Leon Jones Sr. is pastor of Faith Community Church.

    Career school sets grand opening

    Arkansas Employment Career Center (AECC), 3131 W. 28th Ave., in Regency Square, will hold its grand opening at noon Nov. 5 with the Pine Bluff Regional Chamber of Commerce. The agency is licensed by the Arkansas Division of Higher Education, according to a flyer on the Chamber’s newsletter. 

    Friday, Nov. 6

    Extension Homemakers plan Holiday Foods Show

    The Annual Jefferson County Extension Homemakers Holiday Foods Show will be held at 10 a.m. Nov. 6 at St. Joseph Catholic Church, 412 W. Sixth Ave. The doors will open at 9 a.m., according to a news release. Tickets are $25 and are available by texting 870-718-1846 or contacting any Jefferson County Extension Homemaker for assistance in getting tickets. 

    Saturday, Nov. 7

    Free maternal health event set

    The Arkansas Department of Health (ADH) will host free community events to connect expectant and new mothers, families, and members of their support networks with services that support their health and well-being. The Southeast Arkansas event will be held Nov. 7 from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. at Saracen Landing, 200 Lake Saracen Drive. The outdoor event will feature ADH programs, community organizations, playground access, and resources selected for families in Southeast Arkansas. Attendees will learn about maternal and postpartum health, WIC, immunizations, breastfeeding support, family planning, and other services available, according to a news release. A Northwest Arkansas event will be held Oct. 10 from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. at the Jones Center, 922 E. Emma Ave., Springdale. Details: ClaimYourCareAR.com.

    Wednesday, Nov. 11

    Corvettes and Veterans ﻿event set Nov. 11

    Author Jason Irby, a Jefferson County native, announces the  fourth Annual Corvettes and Veterans Salute and Ceremony will be held from 9:30 a.m. to 3 p.m. Nov. 11 at War Memorial Stadium in Little Rock. This ceremony will be free and open to the public, according to Stuff in The Bluff.com. The event is being presented by Irby along with the Jason Irby Innovation Foundation, American Legion, Sequoyah National Research Center, WoodmenLife, Tracey Lynn Foundation, War Memorial Stadium and Slick Auto Care.

    Thursday, Nov. 12

    PB NAACP sets election

    The Pine Bluff Branch of the NAACP will hold an election of officers and at-large members of the executive committee.  The meeting will be held Nov. 12. The election will be administered using the electronic voting platform. Voting will open from 3-7 p.m. 

    Wednesday, Nov. 14

    Lakeside slates Bells and Blessings luncheon

    Lakeside United Methodist Church, 1500 S. Olive St., will present its Bells and Blessings luncheon from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. Nov. 14. Lunch will be served from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. Proceeds benefit the Broadmoor Elementary School Backpack Feeding program, according to a news release. Tickets are $20 each and available from the church office or a Lakeside member. Details: Lakeside: 870-534-6241.

    City announces Veterans Parade

    The second Annual PIne Bluff Regional Veterans Parade will be held at 10 a.m. Nov. 14 in downtown Pine Bluff. Participants are urged to sign up. Details: Mayor Vivian Flowers’s office, 870-730-2003. Sponsors include the city of Pine Bluff, the Pine Bluff Convention Center, ExplorePine Bluff.com, and the American Legion, according to a flyer.

    Through Saturday, Nov. 14Next Gen STEAM Exhibition Series 5 underwayThe ARTX3 Campus will host the Next Gen STEAM Exhibition Series 5 Make a Liquid Float Oct. 15-Nov. 14. Make a Liquid Float is a hands-on science activity that introduces students to density and the properties of liquids. Students combine different liquids and observe how some liquids form separate layers instead of mixing together. They will make predictions, observe the layers, and discover that liquids with different densities can float above or sink below one another. Each Next Gen STEAM Exhibition Series runs for four weeks. Visitors may drop into the Creative Construction Zone during regular visitor hours (10 a.m. to 5 p.m. Tuesday through Friday, and 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. Saturday). 

    Through Friday, Nov. 20

    ARTx3 to host Pine Bluff Art League Exhibition

    The ARTx3 Campus will present the 2026 Pine Bluff Art League Exhibition through Nov. 20 in the Kline Family Foundation Gallery at The ARTSpace on Main, 623 S. Main St. The annual juried exhibition showcases works by members of the Pine Bluff Art League. 

    Saturday, Nov. 21

    Author to hold book signing

    Detria Austin Everson, a nationally recognized leadership speaker and author, will host a book talk and signing at 11 a.m. Nov. 21 at the Pine Bluff Downtown Library, 600 S Main St. Everson is author of Serving Is a Superpower: Transforming Lives Through Service Is the Power of Giving and Receiving. The former Pine Bluff resident  welcomes the community, especially local high school and college students, for what she hopes will be a meaningful and engaging conversation about Serving Is a Superpower and the power each person has to serve others. She rescheduled her event from Sept. 19. To register, visit https://form.jotform.com/261936068377065

    Thursday, Dec. 10

    Future Builders plan summit 

    Future Builders Health Education Summit will be held from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. Dec. 10 at the Wilma Anderson Center, 4701 Dollarway Road. The event is open to the community including healthcare professionals, residents, and students. Topics will include sexual health and prevention, chronic disease and heart health; tobacco, marijuana and substance use; mental and behavioral health; your health and prevention; healthy aging and consumer protection; and health equity, access and community wellness. There will be complimentary breakfast, snacks and lunch, as well as giveaways, networking and community resources. Vendors will be onsite. To register to attend, become a vendor or other details, visit https://form.jotform.com/262738210528053 .

    Friday, Dec. 11

    EASI to observe 25th anniversary

    Emergency Ambulance Service Inc. (EASI) will celebrate its 25th anniversary at 6:30 p.m. Dec. 11 at the Pine Bluff Convention Center. The featured speaker is Melinda Mayo, retired meteorologist from KATV, Channel 7. The attire is semi-formal. The community is invited to celebrate with them, according to a flyer. For sponsorship or ticket information, visit easi.emslearn.org or call 870-536-0734.
