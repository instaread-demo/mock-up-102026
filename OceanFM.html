    Local Authorities facing a ban on the purchase of social homes

    October 20, 2026, 07:01

     

    Listen to audio version of this article
    Sponsored by
    0:00 / 0:45
    Instaread

    The respective county councils in Sligo, Leitrim and Donegal will have to put a much greater emphasis on the construction of new homes from 2028 onwards.

     

    It’s emerged that Local authorities will be banned from buying social homes from private developers from the end of next year.

    Advertisement

     

    Housing Minister James Browne is bringing proposals to cabinet aimed at driving councils to build more homes directly rather than relying on turnkey purchasing.

     

    Browne will tell his colleagues the future of social housing will be led by local authorities and approved housing bodies working with the Land Development Agency.

    Advertisement

     

    The Minister has been critical of councils buying homes to meet their social housing targets that otherwise could have been sold on the private market.
