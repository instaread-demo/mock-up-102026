    Just two 'Above The Shop Grants' have been approved in the Midlands in the last four months.

    Two grants have been recieved in Laois over the third quarter of the year, with one approved.

    While one from the previous four months has gotten the green light in Westmeath, with none gotten in the time period from July to September

    Just 30 grants have been submitted to local authorites across the country, with the payment used for converting or refurbishing vacant space above a commercial premises into residential use.
