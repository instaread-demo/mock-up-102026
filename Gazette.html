Police investigate two indecent exposure incidents in Harlow

Detectives are investigating two indecent exposure incidents at a playing field.

Both incidents occurred at Barn Mead Playing Fields, Harlow.

The first incident happened on September 6 at about 7.30pm on the cycle track at the bottom of the field, near the cut through to Hookfield.

The second instance happened on Tuesday, October 13, at the same location.

Detectives believe the same man is responsible for both incidents.

The suspect is described as being white, in his 20s to 30s, with a slight to medium build, and short dark or dark brown hair.

During the first incident, he was wearing a green jumper and dark-coloured trousers, while in the second, he was seen in dark-coloured jeans and a long black coat.

Police are appealing to the public for any information, CCTV, dashcam, or other footage related to the incidents.

They are asking people to quote the crime reference number 42/164009/26 for all reports.

Reports can be submitted online at www.essex.police.uk/digital101.

Alternatively, people can contact independent charity Crimestoppers anonymously via their website or by calling 0800 555 111.
